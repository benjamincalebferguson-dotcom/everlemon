Why EverLemon
"Grief evolves. Most memorial products don't."

The conventional memorial market is built around the immediate aftermath of loss: urns, plaques, flowers, keepsakes. Meaningful in the short term, but static.

Many families do not feel in year three what they felt in week three. What they seek later is not intensified sorrow, but comfort, continuity, and an occasion to gather.

EverLemon is designed for that arc. Not a product. A living ritual that matures alongside the family.

Trust & Impact

Why you can trust
a long promise

EverLemon is a multi-year commitment. Here's how we honour it.

Orchard provenance

EverLemon works with a partner orchard in the Riverland, South Australia, chosen for its citrus heritage, growing conditions, and long-term reliability.

🌿

Partner orchard network

Every tree is planted and stewarded with a verified partner farm. We maintain quality standards, regular reporting, and transparent communication on how your grove is growing.

📬

Emotionally intelligent updates

We communicate with families at a restrained, thoughtful cadence: seasonal milestones, meaningful moments. Never spam. Always something worth opening.

The Harvest Kit

In November of the third year after ordering, your Harvest Kit arrives. Personalised lemonade made from your tree's fruit, prepared to invite gathering and joy.

51%

The Giving Pledge

EverLemon is committed to directing 51% of distributable net profit, after tax and after prudent operating reserves, to a formally governed giving program supporting small-scale farming partners, hunger-alleviation initiatives, and aligned stewardship outcomes.
